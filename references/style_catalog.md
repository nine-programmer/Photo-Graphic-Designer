# Master Style Catalog & Typography Guide (12 Curated Art Directions)

When the user asks to design a photo graphic, present a curated selection of these **12 distinct design styles** (or offer to generate 3–4 variations simultaneously so they can compare).

---

## 1. โรแมนติก-ดวงดาว (Celestial & Starry Romance)
- **Best For**: Night sky photos, stargazing, camping at night, romantic anniversaries, dreamy portraits.
- **Visual Motifs (SVG)**:
  - Fine double-line gold/champagne outer border with astrolabe/compass corner arcs.
  - Connected constellation lines (`stroke-dasharray="4 3"`) forming subtle heart or zodiac patterns blended into the sky.
  - 4-point and 8-point glowing star sparkles (`<feGaussianBlur>`) and delicate shooting star trails.
  - Crescent moon filigree emblem above the title.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English Eyebrow**: `Cinzel` (`font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: 8px; text-transform: uppercase`)
  - **Thai Main Title**: `Charm` (`font-weight: 700`, metallic champagne-gold CSS gradient `#FFFFFF -> #FFF6DF -> #F0CA86`)
  - **Thai Subtitle & Body**: `Prompt` (`font-weight: 300–400`)
  - **Closing Script**: `Cormorant Garamond` Italic
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Text: `#FFFFFF` / `#FFF8EB`
  - Gold Accent: `#F6E2B3` / `#E8C27D`
  - Backdrop Glow: `rgba(8, 17, 42, 0.42)`

---

## 2. มินิมอล-แมกกาซีน (Minimal Editorial / Kinfolk & Outdoor Postcard)
- **Best For**: Landscape travel shots, camping, coffee/lifestyle, modern architectural or candid couple shots.
- **Visual Motifs (SVG & CSS)**:
  - Crisp `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.38)` editorial postcard frame.
  - Top magazine metadata bar (`SPECIAL EDITION • ARCHIVE`, location tag `KIRIWONG CAMPING`).
  - Vertical architectural accent rule (`border-left: 1.5px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.45)`) anchoring the text block.
  - Minimalist line-art emblem stamp in the bottom corner (tent, pine trees, crescent moon, coordinates).
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **Metadata Header**: `Montserrat` (`font-weight: 500; letter-spacing: 5px`)
  - **English Display**: `Cormorant Garamond` or `Playfair Display` (Regular + Italic highlight)
  - **Thai Headline**: `Noto Serif Thai` (`font-weight: 600`)
  - **Thai Body**: `Prompt` (`font-weight: 300`)
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Text: `#FFFFFF`
  - Editorial Warm Sand Accent: `#F3DFC1`
  - Stamp Container: `rgba(10, 19, 38, 0.55)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`

---

## 3. อบอุ่น-น่ารัก (Warm & Cute Hand-Drawn Doodle)
- **Best For**: Candid couple smiles, cozy camping, family moments, playful & heartfelt birthday posts.
- **Visual Motifs (SVG)**:
  - Rounded sketchy dashed border (`stroke-dasharray="14 10" rx="28"`).
  - Draped string of warm camping fairy lights along the top sky with glowing warm-yellow bulbs.
  - Hand-drawn smiling crescent moon, doodled 5-point stars, and `+` sparkle crosses.
  - Floating coral-pink hearts (`#FF7B93`, `#FFA6B6`) and a curved dashed arrow pointing directly to the subject with a handwritten callout note.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **Top Pill Badge**: `Caveat` (`font-weight: 700` inside a warm cream pill `#FFF5E1` with dark navy text `#26324D`)
  - **Thai Main Title**: `Sriracha` (layered 3D-style navy offset shadow + soft warm glow)
  - **Thai Subtitle, Body & Callout**: `Mali` (`font-weight: 500–700`) inside a dashed translucent rounded box.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Text: `#FFFAF0`
  - Warm Yellow Highlight: `#FFE299`
  - Heart Accent: `#FF7B93`

---

## 4. วินเทจฟิล์มอนาล็อก (Vintage Film & Kodachrome Memory)
- **Best For**: Golden hour, sunset camping, nostalgic road trips, candid laughter.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Classic 35mm film rebate frame or warm off-white gallery mat border (`#F7F3EA`).
  - Subtle warm halation glow and LED-style quartz date stamp (`'26 10 31`) in the corner.
  - Minimalist analog camera viewfinder crop marks (`+` center crosshair and corner brackets).
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Fraunces` or `Libre Baskerville` Italic
  - **Thai**: `Pridi` or `Noto Serif Thai`
  - **Timestamp / Metadata**: `Space Mono` or `Courier Prime`
- **Color Palette**:
  - Cream Matte: `#F7F3EA`, Warm Amber `#FFB347`, Vintage Gold `#FFD166`

---

## 5. โมเดิร์นกลาส-มิวสิคการ์ด (Modern Glassmorphism & Memory Player)
- **Best For**: Gen-Z / modern aesthetic, romantic playlist vibe, sleek social media feeds.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Frosted glass card (`background: rgba(255,255,255,0.12); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.28); border-radius: 24px`).
  - Minimalist Spotify/Apple Music-inspired progress bar (`01:43 ────●────── 03:65`), heart icon, and play/infinity controls.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`
  - **Thai**: `Kanit` or `Anuphan` (`font-weight: 300–600`)

---

## 6. แคมป์ปิ้งเอาท์ดอร์-เรโทรแบดจ์ (Outdoor Adventure & National Park Emblem)
- **Best For**: Tent setups, mountain ridges, forest trails, campfire cooking, adventure couples.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Vintage hexagonal or circular National Park / Adventure patch emblem in SVG (mountain peaks, pine trees, campfire, banner ribbon).
  - Subtle topographic contour lines in the corner or border.
  - Compass rose directional indicator (`N • E • S • W`) and GPS coordinates.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Oswald` or `Bebas Neue` + `Merriweather`
  - **Thai**: `Chonburi` or `Kanit` SemiBold
- **Color Palette**:
  - Campfire Orange `#F4A261`, Pine Cream `#FAF3E0`, Earth Gold `#E9C46A`

---

## 7. มินิมอลญี่ปุ่น-โปสเตอร์ซัคกะ (Japanese Zakka / Muji Editorial Poster)
- **Best For**: Airy skies, green meadows, peaceful morning mist, calm & poetic mood.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Generous asymmetric whitespace or thin Japanese-style vertical/horizontal callout boxes.
  - Delicate fine-line botanical or mountain silhouette (`stroke-width="1"`).
  - Red/Terracotta hanko-inspired seal stamp (`รัก` or `HBD` in a tiny rounded square stamp).
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Shippori Mincho` or `Cormorant`
  - **Thai**: `Sarabun` Light / `Kodchasan` + `Noto Serif Thai`
- **Color Palette**:
  - Pure White `#FFFFFF`, Matcha/Sage Mist `#E2ECE9`, Hanko Coral Stamp `#D9534F`

---

## 8. ซีเนมาติก-มูฟวี่ซับไตเติล (Cinematic Widescreen & Film Credits)
- **Best For**: Wide-angle 3:2 or 16:9 scenic shots with strong storytelling atmosphere.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Subtle cinema letterbox bars (top and bottom black/dark-navy bars `height: 85px`).
  - Movie poster billing block / director's credit in the top or bottom bar (`A FILM BY WANCHALOEM • STARRING MY FAVORITE PERSON`).
  - Classic yellow or warm-white bilingual cinema subtitle at the bottom or upper sky.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **Credits**: `Cinzel` or `Montserrat` Ultra-condensed tracked out
  - **Subtitles**: `Prompt` Regular + `Playfair Display` Italic
- **Color Palette**:
  - Cinema Subtitle Yellow `#FFE066`, Crisp White `#FFFFFF`

---

## 9. โพลารอยด์-สแครปบุ๊ก (Polaroid Collage & Washi Tape Scrapbook)
- **Best For**: Warm personal keepsakes, birthday memory boards, printed-card feel.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Tilted white Polaroid frame or torn paper edge accent with semi-transparent Washi tape strips at the corners.
  - Handwritten marker notes on the Polaroid bottom margin + cute sticker badges.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Permanent Marker` or `Caveat`
  - **Thai**: `Itim` or `Sriracha`

---

## 10. ลักชัวรี-โกลด์ฟอยล์ (Luxury Champagne Gold & Fine Art Gallery)
- **Best For**: Milestone birthdays (30th, 40th), wedding anniversaries, formal or breathtaking scenic portraits.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Double bevel-style metallic gold gradient frame with Art Deco / geometric diamond corner flourishes.
  - Roman numerals or monogram crest emblem.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Cinzel Decorative` + `Pinyon Script`
  - **Thai**: `Charm` + `Noto Serif Thai` Light

---

## 11. โบทานิคอล-สีน้ำธรรมชาติ (Botanical Line Art & Soft Watercolor Glow)
- **Best For**: Lush green campsites, riverside, flowers, daytime nature portraits.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Delicate SVG eucalyptus/fern leaf sprigs framing the typography block.
  - Soft pastel watercolor wash glow behind the text.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Great Vibes` or `Sacramento`
  - **Thai**: `Charmonman` or `Mali`

---

## 12. เฟสติวัล-นีออนแคมป์ (Neon Glow & Night Party Vibe)
- **Best For**: Fun party nights, celebratory toasts, energetic & playful birthday posts.
- **Visual Motifs**:
  - Multi-layered CSS/SVG neon tube glow (`#FF4D8D`, `#00F5D4`, `#FFE66D`) around badges, stars, and icons (cake, cheers glasses, music notes).
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - **English**: `Righteous` or `Outfit`
  - **Thai**: `Mitr` or `Kanit` Bold
